Lifeloch v0.10.10

CIA: dist/Lifeloch-v0.10.10.cia (devkitPro build; hardware testing pending).
Download the raw binary from GitHub, check SHA256SUMS.txt, copy it to the
SD/microSD card of an already modded 3DS, and install with FBI.
Keep sdmc:/3ds/command-deck/ intact when updating to preserve saves.

BUILD_STATUS.txt: artifact checksum and remaining hardware checks.
CHANGELOG.md: release/design history.
LLM-CONTEXT.md: architecture and editing/test checklist.
COPY_AND_BRANDING.md: terminology and artwork permissions.
